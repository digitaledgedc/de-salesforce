User Guide
A step-by-step guide for every stage of your sales process. Each section tells you exactly which button to click and what to fill in.
1
Home & Dashboard
The first screen after you log in
What you see first
The Welcome Card
At the top of your Home screen is a card with your name and a greeting. Below it is your task list for today - anything due today appears here. Overdue tasks show in red. The dark/light toggle in the card switches your display theme and saves automatically.
📋 Today's Tasks
Everything due today is listed here. Click any task title to open the deal or record it belongs to. Overdue tasks appear in red - deal with those first.
🔔 Notifications
The bell icon at the top right shows system alerts - deal review requests, Finance approval decisions, and MEDDPICC reminders. Check it at the start of each day.
💡
Morning habitCheck Home first. Clear any overdue tasks. Open Opportunities and review your pipeline. The whole thing takes 5 minutes if your records are up to date.
2
Navigating the CRM
How to get around and find what you need
📌 The tabs at the top of every page

Leads - new prospects not yet converted to a deal
Accounts - companies you do business with
Contacts - individuals at those companies
Opportunities - your active deals
Quotes - pricing proposals you've built or sent
Contracts - signed agreements linked to closed deals
Orders - what gets delivered after a contract is signed

🔍 Finding anything fast
Click the search bar at the top. Type a company name, a person's name, or an opportunity. Results appear instantly. Click to open.
📌 Set your default list view
Go to Opportunities. Click the list view dropdown next to the list name. Select "My Open Opportunities". Click the pin icon. This view opens by default from now on.
3
Finding & Working a Lead
A new prospect has come in - here's what to do
What is a Lead?
A Lead is a prospect - not yet a deal
Leads come from web forms, marketing campaigns, or are added manually. They sit in a queue until a rep picks them up. At this stage there is no Account, no Contact, and no Opportunity - those are created when you convert. Your job on a Lead is to qualify it: find out if it's a real opportunity worth pursuing.
  • 1
    Open the Leads tab and find the queue
    Click Leads in the top navigation. Click the list view dropdown (next to the list name) and switch to "All Open Leads" or your team's queue view. These are leads waiting to be claimed.
  • 2
    Open a Lead and read it
    Click the lead's name to open it. Read the Company, Lead Source, Product Interest, and any notes already logged in the Activity timeline at the bottom.
  • 3
    Click "Working" to claim it and start qualifying
    Click the Working button in the action bar near the top of the Lead. A form pops up. Fill in everything you know right now:

    Customer Type - the type of customer (Colocation, Hyperscaler, etc.)
    Segment and Sub-Segment
    Target Country - where they want space
    Product Interest - what they're looking for
    Capacity (kW) - power requirement if known
    Notes - anything else relevant

    Clicking Working assigns the lead to you and sets the status to "Working".
  • 4
    Make contact and log the conversation
    Call or email the prospect. After the call, scroll to the Activity section at the bottom of the Lead and click Log a Call. Write what was discussed and what the next step is. Save. This creates a permanent record of every interaction.
  • 5
    Set a follow-up task
    In the Activity section, click New Task. Set a Subject ("Follow up call - [Company]"), a Due Date, assign it to yourself. Save. This task will appear on your Home screen on that date so you don't forget.
  • 6
    Keep qualifying - repeat steps 4 and 5
    Each time you speak to the prospect, log the call and set the next task. Update the lead fields as you learn more. The Activity timeline builds up a full history of every touchpoint. Continue this loop until you know whether this is a real opportunity or not.
ℹ️
Use Send Email too - You can also click Send Email in the activity bar to send an email directly from the Lead record. It gets logged automatically in the timeline - no need to log it separately.
4
Approving or Disqualifying a Lead
Once you've qualified it - here's the decision
✅ It's a real opportunity - click "Sales Approved"

When you're confident this prospect is worth pursuing, click Sales Approved on the Lead. A form opens. Fill in:

GoLive Target Date - required - when they want to go live
Target Site - required - which DE facility they're interested in
Finance Check Status - initial finance assessment
Sales Team - confirm the right team

Save. The Lead status moves to "Sales Approved". You're ready to convert.

❌ Not a real opportunity - click "Disqualify"

If after your conversations it's clear this isn't going anywhere, click Disqualify on the Lead. A form opens. Fill in:

Disqualification Reason - why it's not moving forward
Notes - any extra context

Save. The Lead is closed. This keeps your pipeline clean and gives the team data on why leads don't convert.

⚠️
Don't rush to Sales Approved - Only approve a lead when you've confirmed genuine interest and have a real target date and site in mind. A premature conversion creates noise in the pipeline. If in doubt, keep working the lead.
5
Converting a Lead
Turning a qualified lead into an Account, Contact, and Opportunity
💡
What does Convert do? It creates an Account (the company), a Contact (the person), and one or more Opportunities - one for each target country the prospect mentioned. All three are linked together automatically. After conversion, the Lead is marked "Converted" and you work from the Opportunity forward.
  • 1
    Click Convert on the Lead
    Open the Lead record. Click Convert in the action bar. A guided step-by-step flow opens in a popup. Follow the screens - it walks you through everything.
  • 2
    Check if the company already exists
    The system automatically searches for an Account with the same company name.

    Match found: You'll see the existing Account. Click to link to it - this prevents creating a duplicate company.

    No match: A new Account will be created. Check the spelling of the company name before continuing.
  • 3
    Confirm Account classification
    The flow asks for a few fields that go on the Account record:

    Segment - Enterprise, SMB, Hyperscaler, etc.
    Sub-Segment
    Operation Country
    Sales Team
    Strategic Account - tick if this is a high-priority account
    Tax ID - if available
  • 4
    Review and convert
    The summary screen shows what will be created. Check the Account name, Contact name, and email. Click Convert. The system creates the Account, Contact, and Opportunities automatically.
  • 5
    Open your new Opportunity
    The confirmation screen shows links to everything created. Click the Opportunity link to open it - this is where your deal work begins. If the prospect has multiple target countries, you'll see one Opportunity per country.
✅
After conversion - The Lead is marked Converted. The Account is created as Active. The Contact is linked to the Account. Each Opportunity starts at Stage 1-Suspect. Your next step is inside the Opportunity.

Accounts & Contacts
The company and the people you're dealing with
🏢 Account
The Account is the company. All Contacts, Opportunities, Quotes, Contracts, and Orders belong to an Account. Before any deal can close, the Account needs to pass a Finance and KYC check.
👤 Contact
A Contact is a person at the company. You can have multiple Contacts on one Account - the buyer, the technical evaluator, the legal contact. Link the right people to your Opportunity as the deal progresses.
✅ Account Checklist - what Finance needs before you can close

On the Account record, there's an Account Checklist panel showing readiness gates. All must be green before a contract can be issued. The checklist shows:

Finance Approval - Finance reviews and approves the account
KYC (Know Your Customer) - compliance check
Operation Country - must be populated
Credit Block - must be clear
Account Manager - must be assigned

You can check the status at any time by looking at the checklist on the Account. Red means something is missing or outstanding. Green means done.

The Opportunity Record
Where you track and progress your deal
📑 Tabs inside an Opportunity

When you open an Opportunity, tabs run across the top. Here is what each one is for:

Overview - quick summary and a banner showing exactly what's required at this stage
Capacity & Site - power, space, and location requirements
Pipeline - core deal fields: customer type, sales motion, site details
MEDDPICC - your qualification fields and the Deal Health Panel
Commission & Channel - who gets paid and what percentage
Deal Review & Billing - approval status and billing info

🏥 The Deal Health Panel (MEDDPICC tab)

On the MEDDPICC tab, there's a panel on the right side called the Deal Health Panel. It has two tabs:

External - track the people involved in the decision: who they are, what role they play, what they care about. Fill this in as you learn more about the deal.

Internal - commercial details for internal use: pricing, margin, deal team, splits, booking amounts. Your manager reviews this in deal reviews.

💡
Click Qualify to advance the deal - Once you've had initial conversations and confirmed the prospect's requirements, click Qualify on the Opportunity. This advances the stage to 2-Prospect and asks for the confirmed site, power requirements, and close date. Fill those in and save.

9
Building & Sending a Quote
Creating the pricing proposal for the customer
⚠️
Check these first - Before creating a Quote, the Opportunity must have Decision Criteria filled in and Feasibility Status set to either "Feasible" or "Conditional". If those aren't set, the stage won't advance when the Quote is created.
  • 1
    Click "New Quote" on the Opportunity
    Open the Opportunity. Click New Quote in the action bar. A form opens. Set the Quote Type (required) and the Requested Delivery Date. Save. The Opportunity stage moves to 3-Quote automatically.
  • 2
    Add products to the Quote
    Open the Quote that was just created. Click Add Product. A guided flow opens - search for the product, set the quantity, and confirm the pricing. Add each product or service as a separate line item. Save when done.
  • 3
    Send the Quote to the customer
    On the Quote, click Send Quote to Customer. A flow opens - review the summary and confirm the recipient. The Quote status changes to "Presented". If the Opportunity has Economic Buyer, Decision Process, and Facilities Confirmed all filled in, the stage automatically advances to 4-Negotiate.
ℹ️
Didn't advance to 4-Negotiate? Open the Opportunity. Check that Economic Buyer (a contact), Decision Process (text), and Facilities Confirmed (checkbox ticked) are all filled in. Once they are, you can manually click Advance to Negotiate on the Opportunity if needed.
10
Accepting a Quote (SOF Signed)
The customer has agreed - here's what to do
This is the most important button in the system
Accept Quote (SOF Signed) triggers everything
When you click Accept Quote (SOF Signed), the system does all of the following automatically in one step: sets the Opportunity to 5-Won, syncs the deal value to the Opportunity, creates an Order, and creates a Contract. You do not need to do any of those separately.
  • 1
    Open the Quote and click "Accept Quote (SOF Signed)"
    Open the Quote the customer agreed to. Click Accept Quote (SOF Signed) in the action bar. A guided flow opens.
  • 2
    Select or create the Master Service Agreement
    The flow asks whether there is an existing MSA with this customer or whether a new one needs to be created. Select the right option and fill in the contract details the flow requests.
  • 3
    Confirm and complete the flow
    Review the summary screen. Click Confirm. The system automatically: sets Quote Status to "Accepted", sets Opportunity Stage to "5-Won", syncs MRC/NRC/Total Contract Value to the Opportunity, and creates the Order and Contract.
✅
After acceptance - Your manager is notified. Finance and Operations receive alerts. The Order and Contract appear on the Account. Your pipeline shows the deal as Won. Make sure your Account and Contact records are complete - the handoff teams use that data.
11
Contracts & Orders
What happens after the Quote is accepted
ℹ️
Both are created automatically - When you accept a Quote, the system creates both the Contract and the Order without you having to do it manually. Your job is to confirm the Order details and submit it for approval.
📋 The Contract

The Contract is the signed legal agreement with the customer. It's linked to the Account and captures the agreed terms, start date, and duration. You can find it on the Account record under the Contracts related list, or by clicking Contracts in the top navigation.

The Contract is created automatically when the Quote is accepted. If it needs to be revised (e.g. terms change), you can do that from within the Contract record.

📦 The Order - confirm and submit

The Order is the fulfillment record - it tells Operations what to deliver and Finance what to bill. After Quote acceptance, the Order is created automatically. You need to do two things:

1. Confirm the Order: Open the Order. Click Confirm Order. Review the details - make sure the products and dates are correct. Save.

2. Submit for Approval: Click Submit for Approval. This sends the Order to Finance and Operations for review. You'll get a notification when it's approved.

After that, Operations takes over delivery. You may be contacted if there are questions about the order details.

🔍 Finding your Contracts
Click Contracts in the top navigation, or go to the Account record and scroll to the Contracts related list. You'll see all contracts linked to that customer.
📦 Finding your Orders
Click Orders in the top navigation, or find them on the Account or Contract record in the Orders related list.

Common Questions
Things that come up when you're getting started
I can't find the Working / Sales Approved / Convert button
These buttons are in the action bar at the top of the Lead record, just below the record name and highlights panel. If they're not visible, look for a "More" dropdown at the end of the button bar - some actions are hidden there when there isn't enough screen space to show them all.
The stage won't change when I try to advance it
Stages in Digital Edge have gate requirements. Look at the Overview tab on the Opportunity - there's a banner at the top showing exactly what's missing for the current stage. Fill in those fields and try again. Common missing fields are: Decision Criteria, Feasibility Status, Economic Buyer, Decision Process, and Facilities Confirmed.
I converted a Lead but I can't find the Opportunity
Go to the Opportunities tab and search for the company name. The Opportunity name is usually "[Company Name] - [Target Country]". Alternatively, go to the Account that was created and scroll to the Opportunities related list - it will be there.
I sent a Quote but the Opportunity didn't advance to 4-Negotiate
Three fields on the Opportunity must be filled in for this to happen: Economic Buyer (a linked Contact), Decision Process (text describing how they make decisions), and Facilities Confirmed (a checkbox that must be ticked). Open the Opportunity, fill those in, then the stage can advance - either automatically or by clicking Advance to Negotiate manually.
Should I create a new Account, or use an existing one?
Always use an existing Account if one exists. Search for the company name before creating anything. Duplicate Accounts cause problems for Finance and reporting. If you're unsure, ask your manager before creating a new one.
The customer is in multiple countries - do I need multiple Opportunities?
The system handles this automatically during Lead conversion. When you convert a Lead, it creates one Opportunity per target country from the lead record. You don't need to create them manually.
How do I log a call or meeting?
Open the record you want to log against (Lead, Opportunity, Account, or Contact). Scroll to the Activity section. Click Log a Call. Fill in the Subject and a description of what was discussed. Save. It appears in the timeline permanently and is visible to anyone who opens the record.
I accepted the Quote but I can't find the Contract or Order
Go to the Account record and scroll down. The Contracts and Orders related lists will show everything created automatically by the acceptance. You can also click Contracts or Orders in the top navigation to search for them by account name.
